📖 Fractions Master Notes & Concepts Hub

A fraction represents parts of a whole or collection. Review the core definitions, naming rules, division relations, mixed numbers, and operations below.

1. Anatomy & Naming of Fractions

\[\frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}} = \frac{\text{Parts we are looking at}}{\text{Total equal parts in a whole}}\]

• Denominator: Number of equal parts in a whole.

• Numerator: Number of parts considered.

• Naming: Most denominators add "-th". Exceptions: 2 = half, 3 = third, 4 = quarter/fourth.

2. Fractions as Division

\[a \div b = \frac{a}{b}\]

A fraction bar is another way of writing division. For example, dividing 4 items among 5 parts is written as \(\frac{4}{5}\).

3. Improper Fractions & Mixed Numbers

• Improper Fraction: Numerator \(\ge\) Denominator (e.g., \(\frac{4}{3}\)).

• Mixed Number: A whole number and a proper fraction combined (e.g., \(1\frac{1}{3}\)).

4. Adding & Subtracting Fractions

• Like Denominators: Add or subtract the numerators while keeping the denominator the same:

\[\frac{a}{c} + \frac{b}{c} = \frac{a+b}{c}\]
